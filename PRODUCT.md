# ComicToons
ComicToons helps streamers and moderators give late arrivals immediate context, then keep the best moments as a comic recap issue. The demo runs locally on invented chat with no API keys; Twitch connection is a future integration.

The studio answers: what moment should I approve now? The overlay answers: what did I miss? The recap answers: what made this stream worth remembering?

Voice: an editor with a sense of humor; direct controls, original characters, honest sample labels. The creator always has approval and panic-hide controls. Generated art is protected: full16:9 frames, all lettering in external expanding rails.
